# 1.8.2+32

Date: 2026-10-30. Type: MINOR (asked for as a minor change). Previous: [[99 - Changelog/1.8.1+31|1.8.1+31]].

## What changed
- Each resolver has a retry button next to Edit. It requests only what that resolver is missing and shows the progress bar ([[03 - Features/Certificates|Certificates]]).
- [[06 - API/API Overview|API Overview]] now lists the retry and hard refresh endpoints.

## Tested
Unit tests in [refresh_test.go](../../server/internal/api/refresh_test.go). On the dev environment with Pebble: a host that failed while Pebble was unreachable got its certificate after a retry, 6 of 6 on the bar; no restart when nothing is missing; refused for an unknown or unapplied resolver. `dev/test.py`: 30 of 30 fresh, 28 of 28 repeat. Not run: `dev/test_npm.py`, a real Let's Encrypt request, and the button and windows in a browser (type check and build only). Image published in [[99 - Changelog/1.8.3+33|1.8.3+33]].
